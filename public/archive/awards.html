MMSPL Honorary Members

In order to permanently recognize individuals who, through personal involvement with the league, have contributed to its growth, organization or enjoyment by its members, the league offers a status of honourary membership.

This membership entitles the individual to participate in all social activities of the league that would otherwise be restricted to league members.

In order to maintain this status an individual must:

  • No longer be an active player in the MMSPL
  • Must be nominated for the position at the Annual General Meeting
  • Must be accepted by a majority vote at the Annual General Meeting
