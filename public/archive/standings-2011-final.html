Final
The home team for Round Robin games is the highest seed from the Regular Season Standings
L1 = Level 1 Qualifer (Best record within your box), game times selected by lot
L2 = Level 2 Qualifer (1-4 best records of Non Box Winners), game times selected by lot
L3 = Level 3 Qualifer (5-8 best records of Non Box Winners), game times selected by lot
Teams finishing with the 13-16th round-robin records will be eliminated
Wild Card (WC) Games may not be against teams from the same box; L3 will be redrawn
Quarter Final (QF) Games may be against teams from the same box
Semi-Final (SF) Games and The Jim McGregor Final - The home team will be the higher Level Qualifer*
*If both teams are on par, the home team will be the higher Regular Season Seed

For full details, consult sections 10 and 11 of the 2011 MMSPL House Rules
